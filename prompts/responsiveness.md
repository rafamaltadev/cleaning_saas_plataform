## GLOBAL RESPONSIVENESS (MANDATORY FOR ENTIRE APPLICATION)

This applies to ALL product screens (dashboard, chat, scheduling, CRM, settings, etc).
The application MUST be mobile-first.

---

## CORE PRINCIPLE

The user must be able to run their business entirely from a smartphone.
If a task cannot be completed on mobile, the design is invalid.

---

## BREAKPOINTS

- Mobile: up to 640px
- Tablet: 641px – 1024px
- Desktop: above 1024px

---

## NAVIGATION

- Mobile:
  - Bottom navigation OR collapsible sidebar
  - Maximum 5 primary actions
  - Always reachable with thumb

- Desktop:
  - Sidebar navigation
  - Expanded menu

---

## DASHBOARD

- Mobile:
  - Vertical stacking of cards
  - Prioritize key metrics only
  - Quick actions at top

- Desktop:
  - Grid layout
  - Full analytics view

---

## CHAT / AUTOMATION (CRITICAL)

- Mobile:
  - WhatsApp-like interface
  - Full-width chat
  - Input always visible
  - Fast actions (reply, templates, automation)

- Desktop:
  - Split view (conversations + details)

---

## SCHEDULING

- Mobile:
  - List view (default)
  - Simple calendar toggle
  - Large tap targets

- Desktop:
  - Full calendar grid

---

## FORMS / CRM

- Mobile:
  - Single column
  - Progressive disclosure (step-by-step)

- Desktop:
  - Multi-column allowed

---

## TABLES / DATA

- Mobile:
  - Avoid complex tables
  - Use cards or collapsible rows

- Desktop:
  - Full tables allowed

---

## BUTTONS & INTERACTIONS

- Minimum touch size: 44px
- No hover-dependent interactions
- Immediate feedback on tap

---

## PERFORMANCE

- Fast loading on mobile networks
- Avoid heavy animations
- Lazy loading where needed

---

## FORBIDDEN

- Horizontal scroll
- Hidden actions
- Desktop-only features
- Tiny click targets
- Complex multi-column forms on mobile

---

## FINAL RULE

Mobile is NOT a simplified version.

Mobile is the primary version.
Desktop is the enhancement.
